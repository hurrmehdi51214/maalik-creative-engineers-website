Unmanned Systems and Counter-UAS

Extending reach without extending exposure, and denying the same advantage to an adversary.

Discuss a requirement Request a capability statement

Domain overview

Extending reach without extending exposure, and denying the same advantage to an adversary.

Unmanned systems have changed the arithmetic of reconnaissance and of precision effect. They have also handed the same advantage to every adversary, which is why this domain is written as two halves that must be procured together.

On the platform side we supply loitering and ISR fixed-wing systems, compact VTOL reconnaissance quadcopters, tactical FPV platforms, and the electro-optical, infrared, radar and communications payloads they carry, including arrangements for technology transfer and local co-production.

On the counter side, detection, identification and defeat are three separate procurement problems. A system that detects but cannot positively identify will generate engagements against friendly and civil traffic. We supply and integrate all three.

Integration

This domain does not work alone.

A sensor that does not report into a command system is an observation, not a capability. We design and deliver the connections as deliberately as we deliver the equipment.

Systems here connect most often to ISR, Air Defence, Electronic Warfare. We design and deliver those connections as part of the same program.

Support and sustainment

Availability is decided after delivery, not before it.

Every system we supply comes with a support arrangement, not an assumption of one. That means initial spares provisioning, in-country service capability, operator and maintainer training delivered in English, warranty administration, structured post-warranty support, and access to depot level repair, overhaul and life extension where the platform requires it.
