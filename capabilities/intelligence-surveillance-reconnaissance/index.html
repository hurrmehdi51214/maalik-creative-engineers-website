Domain overview

Seeing further, earlier, and in conditions that defeat the unaided eye.

ISR is not a category of equipment. It is the process of converting dispersed observation into a single picture that a commander can act on. This domain covers the sensors that observe and the architecture that fuses them.

We supply airborne, ground, maritime and sub-surface sensing, from podded reconnaissance systems and multi-mode airborne radar to man-portable ground surveillance radar, coastal surface search sets, hull-mounted and towed sonar, and fixed underwater surveillance for harbours and approaches.

The work that matters is rarely the sensor alone. It is cueing one sensor from another and delivering the result into a command system in a form that can be used.

Integration

This domain does not work alone.

A sensor that does not report into a command system is an observation, not a capability. We design and deliver the connections as deliberately as we deliver the equipment.

Systems here connect most often to Electro-Optics & Night Vision, Unmanned Systems & C-UAS, Command, Control & Communications. We design and deliver those connections as part of the same programme.

Support and sustainment

Availability is decided after delivery, not before it.

Every system we supply comes with a support arrangement, not an assumption of one. That means initial spares provisioning, in-country service capability, operator and maintainer training delivered in English, warranty administration, structured post-warranty support, and access to depot level repair, overhaul and life extension where the platform requires it.
